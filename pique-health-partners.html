Pique Health Partners is the investment and advisory practice of Michael "Meech" — an operator and angel investor focused exclusively on the performance wellness space.

The thesis is simple: the next decade of consumer health will be won by brands with trusted distribution, not just better products. PHP invests, advises, and connects at that exact junction.
